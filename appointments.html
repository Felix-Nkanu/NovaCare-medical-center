Before Your Visit

Appointment Guidelines

To ensure a smooth and efficient experience, please review the following guidelines before arriving for your appointment.

Arrive Early

Please arrive at least 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment for registration.

Bring Identification

Bring a valid means of identification along with any insurance or medical documents.

Medical Records

Carry previous prescriptions, laboratory results, or referral letters if available.

Current Medications

Inform your doctor about all medications and supplements you are currently taking.

Follow Instructions

Follow any fasting or preparation instructions given before your appointment.

Reschedule if Needed

If you cannot attend, kindly notify us at least 24 hours before your appointment.
